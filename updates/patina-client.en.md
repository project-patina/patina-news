# Patina Client: 16 built-in mods

Patina 0.4.5 makes the client much bigger, the same on Fabric and in the Rust client.

- New mods: AppleSkin, Shulker Tooltip, Speedometer, Zoom, Ping, Perspective, Horse Stats, Status Effects, Weather Changer, Death Info and No Fog
- Each mod has its own switch, settings and reset
- Zoom (C) and Perspective (V) work on any key, held or toggled
- A searchable catalogue of mods on the Patina Client screen
- A smoother loading animation

This is only the start: work on the client continues. More mods and fixes will come in the next updates.
